Emote Player

Privacy policy

Emote Player, by Mostafa Magdy, plays music you add and uses audio analysis to suggest mood playlists. You can use it without an account.

Music and information on your device

The app uses the files or folders you select and reads information such as track titles, artists, duration, and artwork. It keeps your library, playlists, playback state, and analysis results in app storage. On iOS, it can keep a copy of imported music so it can play the file again.

Emote Player does not send your music, library, listening history, or analysis results to the developer. It does not use advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs.

Your choices and deletion

Playback behavior is used to tune mood suggestions only if you enable it. You can disable it or reset mood tuning in Settings. Removing a track from the library leaves the original file in place. App data stays in app storage until you remove it or delete the app. Device backup and any file provider you choose are governed by those services.

Support requests

If you contact the developer, the information you choose to send is used to respond to your request. You can use the same contact form to ask for deletion of information from your support request.
