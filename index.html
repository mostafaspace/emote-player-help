Support

Get help with your music library, mood playlists, and playback.

Add music

Open Library, tap the plus button, and choose files or a folder. Songs appear as they import. Mood analysis runs separately, so you can play your music while it finishes.

Find a song by mood

Choose a mood and tap a song to play it, or play the entire queue. If your mood list is empty, check analysis progress in Library. Connect your device to power if analysis is paused.

Get help

Include “Emote Player,” your app version, phone model, and what happened. Leave out your music files and personal library details.
